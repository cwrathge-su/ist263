Living LBI

LBI has a variety of attractions and things to do. From museums to mini golf, parasailing, and delicious restaurants, LBI has something for everyone.

History and Culture

Tuckerton Seaport & Baymen’s Museum

  • Learn about the history, art, and environment of Barnegat Bay and Pinelands
  • Also offers boat rides and scenic tours
Address: 120 W Main St, Tuckerton, NJ 08087
Phone: 609-296-8868

New Jersey Maritime Museum

Learn about maritime history and view artifacts from the entire state of New Jersey Located in Beach Haven Address: 528 Dock Rd, Beach Haven, New Jersey, 08008 Phone: 609-492-0202

Recreation

Waterpark with a mini golf course for all ages, with body slides, racer slides, a lazy river, and more Open seasonally during summer Address: 300 Taylor Ave, Beach Haven, NJ 08008 Phone: 609-492-4200

Doubling as a spot for food and drinks and a recreation destination, the Boatyard is a spot for casual meals, lawn games, and great views of the bay. Docking for boats is available Address: 2304 E Bay Ave, Manahawkin, NJ 08050 Phone: 609-494-1371

Causeway Marina Boat Rentals

Skiff, pontoon, and kayak rentals Baithouse with fishing essentials also available Address: 2304 E Bay Ave, Manahawkin, NJ 08050 Phone: 609-494-1371

Fantasy Island
