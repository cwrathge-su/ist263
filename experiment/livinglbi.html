Living LBI

LBI has a variety of attractions and things to do. From museums to mini golf, parasailing, and delicious restaurants, LBI has something for everyone.

History and Culture

Tuckerton Seaport & Baymen’s Museum

  • Learn about the history, art, and environment of Barnegat Bay and Pinelands
  • Also offers boat rides and scenic tours

Address: 120 W Main St, Tuckerton, NJ 08087
Phone: 609-296-8868

New Jersey Maritime Museum

  • Learn about maritime history and view artifacts from the entire state of New Jersey
  • Located in Beach Haven

Address: 528 Dock Rd, Beach Haven, New Jersey, 08008
Phone: 609-492-0202

Recreation

Thundering Surf Waterpark

  • Waterpark with a mini golf course for all ages, with body slides, racer slides, a lazy river, and more
  • Open seasonally during summer

Address: 300 Taylor Ave, Beach Haven, NJ 08008
Phone: 609-492-4200

South End Surf N Paddle

  • Kids surf camp, surf lessons for kids and adults
  • Stand-up paddleboard tours available as well

Address: 220 S Bay Ave, Beach Haven, NJ
Phone: 609-492-8823

The Boatyard

  • Doubling as a spot for food and drinks and a recreation destination, the Boatyard is a spot for casual meals, lawn games, and great views of the bay.
  • Docking for boats is available

Address: 2304 E Bay Ave, Manahawkin, NJ 08050
Phone: 609-494-1371

Causeway Marina Boat Rentals

  • Skiff, pontoon, and kayak rentals
  • Baithouse with fishing essentials also available

Address: 2304 E Bay Ave, Manahawkin, NJ 08050
Phone: 609-494-1371

LBI Parasail

  • Enjoy the views of LBI and go parasailing
  • 8am – 8pm, Memorial Day through Labor Day

Address: Lighthouse Marina, W 6th St, Barnegat Light, NJ 08006
Phone: 609-361-6100

Fantasy Island

  • Amusement park located in Beach Haven
  • Rides, arcade, and fun for the whole family

Address: 750 N Bay Ave, Beach Haven, NJ 08008
Phone: 609-492-4000

Shopping

Bay Village

  • 23 shops and eateries including apparel, pizza, gifts, and more
  • Near Fantasy Island and neighboring waterpark

Address: 830 N Bay Ave, Beach Haven, NJ United States 08008

Ron Jon Surf Shop

  • The “Original” Ron Jon Surf Shop, which opened in 1961
  • A must-see destination for visiting LBI, with clothes, surfboards, and everything you need for the beach

Address: 201 9th and Central Avenue, Ship Bottom, NJ 08008
Phone: 609-494-8844

Farias Surf Shop

  • An authentic surf shop with products from brands like RVCA and Jetty
  • Locals recommend Farias as one of the best places to shop on the island

Address: 2800 Long Beach Blvd, Ship Bottom, NJ 08008
Phone: 609-494-7368

Surf City 5 & 10

  • Another local favorite, the best place to go on any rainy day
  • Toys, taffy, puzzles, and all the fun items to keep you occupied

Address: 411 Long Beach Blvd, Surf City, NJ 08008
Phone: 609-494-1872

B & B Department Store

  • Hidden gem of clothes and beach gear located next to Ron Jon
  • Great products without all of the tourist traffic

Address: 901 Central Ave, Ship Bottom, NJ 08008
Phone: 609-361-0760
